The Inventors

Nobel Prize in Physics 2014
Name Key Contribution Year
Isamu Akasaki Pioneered GaN crystal growth and p-type GaN with Amano 1980s
Hiroshi Amano Grew high-quality GaN on sapphire using an AlN buffer layer 1986
Shuji Nakamura Made the first bright, efficient blue LED at Nichia 1993

Why Did So Many Give Up?

Warning: In the 1980s most of the industry bet on zinc selenide (ZnSe) instead of GaN. ZnSe crystals were easier to grow, but the devices failed quickly. Only a few teams kept trying GaN.

Quick Facts

  • Red LED invented in 1962 by Nick Holonyak Jr.
  • First bright blue LED: 1993
  • Nobel Prize: 2014

Where Do We Use Blue LEDs Today?

  1. White LED bulbs (blue LED + yellow phosphor)
  2. Phone, TV and monitor screens
  3. Blu-ray players
  4. Water and air sterilisation (UV relatives of blue LEDs)
