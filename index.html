Introduction

Red LEDs appeared in 1962. Green followed soon after. Yet blue took another three decades. Without blue, there is no white LED light, no bright full-colour screen and no Blu-ray disc.

The Short Answer

It was not truly impossible, but it looked that way.
Three problems had to be solved at once:
a good material, good crystals, and a p-type layer.

Gallium nitride is written GaN, and the blue light it makes has a wavelength of about 450 nm.
